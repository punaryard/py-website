What happens on your site

A building comes down in a fixed order, so that useful material comes out whole and only true waste becomes rubble.

  1. Survey

    We walk the building, measure it and note every door, grille, beam and tile worth saving.

  2. Strip out

    Doors, windows, grilles, fans, wiring, sanitary ware and tiles come out by hand first.

  3. Take down

    Floor by floor, top first. Steel is cut out of each slab before the concrete is broken.

  4. Sort and weigh

    Bricks, steel, wood, metal scrap and rubble go into separate lots. Each lot is weighed on a weighbridge.

  5. Clean plot

    Rubble is carted away for recycling, the ground is levelled and you get a waste disposal certificate.

Three things we do

Use one, or let us run the whole job.

Full building dismantling

Houses, shops, godowns and old factories up to four floors. We bring the crew, machines and safety gear, deal with the municipal paperwork and leave the plot level.

  • Trained crew with helmets, harnesses and a site safety plan
  • Neighbour walls and trees protected
  • Waste disposal certificate at the end

Salvage valuation and buying

Before anything is broken, we list what your building is worth as material. Steel, wood, doors, grilles, tiles and fittings are weighed and priced at the day's market rate.

  • Itemised list before work starts
  • Weighbridge slip for every truck
  • The value is taken off your bill or paid to you

Reused yard and rubble recycling

Good doors, windows, beams and bricks go to our yard and are sold again to people building on a budget. Broken concrete is crushed into aggregate for roads and filling.

  • Buy reused material at a fair price
  • Rubble kept out of rivers and empty plots
  • Less new material dug and burnt

You decide who keeps the material

Every job starts with one choice. We write it into the agreement before work starts.

Keep

You keep all the material.

You pay the full dismantling fee. Doors, steel and bricks are stacked on site or delivered to you. Good if you are rebuilding and want to reuse your own material.

Share

We sell it and split the money with you.

We sell the salvage and take an agreed share to cover our work. Your bill drops by what the material is worth. You see every weighbridge slip and sale price. Most owners choose this.

Sell

We buy it all up front.

We quote one price for the whole building's material and take it off the dismantling fee. One number, no waiting for sales. Good when you want it simple.

Seven steps, in writing

You get a note at every step, with photos. Nothing moves to the next step without your OK.

  1. Survey

    Free site visit. We measure and list reusable material.

  2. Quote

    Written price for the job, the salvage value and your chosen deal.

  3. Permits

    We help with the demolition permission from your municipality or development authority.

  4. Dismantle

    Strip out by hand, then take down floor by floor.

  5. Sort

    Each lot is tagged, photographed and weighed on a weighbridge.

  6. Move

    Lots go to buyers, our yard or the recycling plant. Rubble never goes to a drain or an empty plot.

  7. Close

    Level plot, final account of every rupee, waste disposal certificate.
